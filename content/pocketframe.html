Pocketframe

A little camera.
A shutter in your pocket.

Keep the moment.
Check the frame.

I’m building a small camera that saves photos to its own SD card and works on its own. A StickS3 becomes a wireless shutter and a pocket screen for checking what I’ve captured. The first version will run from a USB power bank; a printed enclosure, physical viewfinder and LiPo power come as the build develops.

About these images and this preview

The hero is an AI-generated concept and the 3D view is an editable draft, not a completed Pocketframe. The planned hardware is an ESP32-CAM with its ESP32-CAM-MB USB dock and an M5Stack StickS3 remote. Hardware photographs are credited reference images used with permission, not photographs of Trevor’s completed build. Firmware behavior is proposed; the screen demonstration is simulated.
